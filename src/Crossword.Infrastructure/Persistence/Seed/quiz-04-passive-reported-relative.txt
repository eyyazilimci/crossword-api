rs-01 | reported-speech | B2 | "I will call you tomorrow," she said. → She said she ___ me the next day. | will call | calls | would call | has called | C | Backshift: will, would olur. tomorrow da the next day olur.
rs-02 | reported-speech | B2 | He asked me where ___. | I lived | did I live | do I live | I am living | A | Aktarılan sorularda düz cümle sırası kullanılır (do/did kalkar) ve zaman bir geri gider: where I lived.
rs-03 | reported-speech | B2 | "Don't touch anything," the officer told us. → The officer told us ___ anything. | don't touch | not touch | to not touching | not to touch | D | Olumsuz emir aktarılırken: tell someone not to + V.
rs-04 | reported-speech | B2 | She asked me ___ I had finished the report. | that | whether | what | did | B | Evet/hayır soruları if ya da whether ile aktarılır.
rs-05 | reported-speech | B2 | "I'm tired," he said yesterday. → Yesterday he said that he ___ tired. | is | was | has been | will be | B | Backshift: am, was olur.
rs-06 | reported-speech | C1 | "I saw her yesterday," he told me. → He told me he ___ her the day before. | has seen | would see | sees | had seen | D | Past Simple aktarılırken Past Perfect olur. yesterday da the day before olur.
rs-07 | reported-speech | B2 | She asked me how long ___ in Ankara. | I had lived | had I lived | did I live | have I lived | A | Aktarılan soruda düz sıra: how long I had lived.
rs-08 | reported-speech | B2 | "Can you help me?" she asked. → She asked if I ___ help her. | may | will | could | did | C | Backshift: can, could olur.
rs-09 | reported-speech | C1 | He apologised for ___ late. | being | to be | be | was | A | apologise for + V-ing.
rs-10 | reported-speech | B2 | The teacher asked us ___ our books. | open | opening | that we open | to open | D | Rica ve emir aktarılırken: ask someone to + V.
rs-11 | reported-speech | C1 | "You should see a doctor," she said to me. → She advised me ___ a doctor. | seeing | that see | to see | see | C | advise someone to + V.
rs-12 | reported-speech | B2 | "Have you ever been to Japan?" he asked. → He asked me if I ___ to Japan. | have ever been | had ever been | ever went | was ever | B | Backshift: Present Perfect, Past Perfect olur.
rs-13 | reported-speech | B2 | "We're leaving tomorrow," they said last month. → They said they were leaving ___. | tomorrow | yesterday | the day before | the following day | D | Zaman ifadesi de kayar: tomorrow, the next day / the following day olur.

rc-01 | relative-clauses | B1 | The man ___ car was stolen called the police. | who | which | whom | whose | D | İyelik (arabası çalınan adam): whose.
rc-02 | relative-clauses | B1 | The book ___ I bought yesterday is very interesting. | who | which | whose | where | B | Nesneler için which (ya da that).
rc-03 | relative-clauses | B2 | The project, ___ was completed ahead of schedule, won an award. | that | what | which | whose | C | Virgülle ayrılan (non-defining) cümleciklerde that kullanılmaz: which.
rc-04 | relative-clauses | B2 | The meeting was cancelled, ___ was a relief to everyone. | which | that | what | it | A | Önceki cümlenin tamamını niteleyen cümlecik: , which.
rc-05 | relative-clauses | B1 | This is the hotel ___ we stayed last summer. | which | who | where | whose | C | Yer bildiren ve edat almayan cümlecik: where (= in which).
rc-06 | relative-clauses | B2 | She's the woman ___ I told you about. | who | which | whose | where | A | Kişiler için who.
rc-07 | relative-clauses | B2 | I don't understand ___ he said. | which | what | that | who | B | what = the thing that (söylediği şey).
rc-08 | relative-clauses | C1 | The candidates, most of ___ had several years of experience, were interviewed. | who | which | them | whom | D | Edattan sonra kişiler için whom: most of whom.
rc-09 | relative-clauses | B2 | Do you remember the day ___ we first met? | when | which | who | whose | A | Zaman bildiren cümlecik: when.
rc-10 | relative-clauses | C1 | The reason ___ he left the company is still unclear. | which | what | why | where | C | the reason why: sebep bildiren kalıp.
rc-11 | relative-clauses | B2 | My sister, ___ lives in Berlin, is a doctor. | that | which | whom | who | D | Virgüllü cümlecik + kişi + özne konumu: who (that kullanılmaz).
rc-12 | relative-clauses | C1 | That's the colleague with ___ I share an office. | who | whom | that | which | B | Edattan sonra kişi için whom gelir (with whom). Edattan sonra that gelemez.
rc-13 | relative-clauses | B2 | Everything ___ she said was true. | what | whose | that | who | C | everything, nothing, all gibi kelimelerden sonra that tercih edilir. what kullanılmaz.
rc-14 | relative-clauses | C1 | He failed the exam, ___ surprised everyone. | which | that | what | who | A | Önceki cümlenin tamamını niteleyen: , which.
rc-15 | relative-clauses | B2 | The children ___ parents work late stay at the after-school club. | who | which | whom | whose | D | İyelik (ebeveynleri geç saate kadar çalışan çocuklar): whose.
rc-16 | relative-clauses | C1 | Is there anything ___ I can do to help? | what | that | who | whose | B | anything'den sonra that kullanılır.
rc-17 | relative-clauses | B2 | The city ___ I was born has changed a lot. | where | which | when | who | A | Yer bildiren cümlecik: where (= in which).
rc-18 | relative-clauses | C1 | I have two brothers, both of ___ are engineers. | who | them | whom | which | C | both of / some of / most of + whom (kişiler için).
